About Calm

Everyday essentials. Industrial ambition.

Founded in South Africa, Calm Tissue proved its products in a competitive retail market before bringing its manufacturing vision home to Botswana.

Our journey

Built in the market.
Back home by purpose.

  1. 2024
    South Africa

    The Calm brand takes shape

    Calm Tissue begins trading in South Africa, learning directly from consumers and the demands of established retail.

  2. Market proven
    Retail experience

    Products meet customers where they shop

    The brand reaches customers through channels including SPAR, Makro Online, Uber Eats and other chain stores—building practical market knowledge and confidence.

  3. 2025
    Coming home

    Calm comes home to Botswana

    Calm Tissue (Pty) Ltd is established in Botswana and earns the Brand Botswana Pride Mark.

  4. 2026
    SPEDU

    Rooted in Selibe Phikwe

    Calm Tissue becomes a fully registered SPEDU client and opens its Selibe Phikwe facility.

  5. 2026
    Recognition

    Third Prize, Local Manufacturing

    Calm Tissue is awarded Third Prize in the Local Manufacturing category at the Botswana Consumer Fair 2026.

Retailer names describe historical sales channels and do not imply endorsement.
